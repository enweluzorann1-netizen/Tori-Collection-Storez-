Ordering & delivery

We aim to keep shopping clear and straightforward. Please read the details before completing your order.

How to place an order

  • Send a screenshot, product image or video to our WhatsApp DM.
  • Confirm product availability, price and any size or colour options before paying.
  • Ask us to confirm your order total and delivery details.
  • Keep your payment confirmation until your order is delivered.

Payment & returns

  • The store's stated policy is payment before delivery.
  • Please confirm availability before making payment.
  • The seller states that payments are non-refundable; contact the store to clarify any issue before ordering.
  • Do not share your banking PIN, password or one-time passcode with anyone.

Delivery

  • Nationwide delivery is available.
  • Delivery fees, estimated timing and dispatch arrangements should be confirmed before payment.
  • Provide an accurate recipient name, phone number and delivery address privately during order confirmation.

Wholesale & retail

  • We serve wholesale buyers, retail customers and end users.
  • Ask us directly about bulk quantities, available stock and current pricing.
  • Prices and availability are confirmed individually; this website does not display unverified prices.
