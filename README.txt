TORI COLLECTION STOREZ WEBSITE
================================

Included:
- index.html: responsive, mobile-friendly storefront website.
- README.txt: setup and publishing notes.

How to preview:
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open index.html in a web browser.

How to publish:
- Upload index.html to a static hosting service such as Netlify, GitHub Pages, or your existing hosting provider.
- To use a custom domain, purchase/connect the domain through your chosen provider.
- Replace the sample hero emblem with the final logo image if desired.
- Add real product photos, prices, stock status, delivery fees, and business email once supplied.
- Test the WhatsApp link and payment details before sharing the website publicly.

Email access invitation:
The site package does not have a hosting account attached, so no invitation can be sent to enweluzorogonnav@gmail.com from this file alone. After choosing/creating the hosting account, invite that address through the provider's team/collaborator settings. Do not share your account password.
